Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy covers email-sender, a personal application written and operated by Ahmed Benhmida (ahmedbenhmida.com). It is used solely by its author to send his own job-application emails from his own Gmail account.

Who operates this application

Ahmed Benhmida, Tunis, Tunisia. Contact: ahmedbenhamida290@gmail.com.

There are no other users

email-sender is not a product and is not offered to anyone else. It has a single user — its author — acting on his own Google Account. It collects no information from visitors to this website, and it has no sign-up, no accounts and no customers.

What Google data it accesses, and why

The application requests the https://mail.google.com/ scope on the author's own Google Account. That scope is required because the application uses Gmail's SMTP and IMAP interfaces, which do not accept narrower scopes. It is used for exactly two purposes:

  • Sending — delivering the author's own outgoing messages through Gmail's SMTP server.
  • Reading delivery notifications — scanning the author's own mailbox over IMAP for bounce messages from mail servers, so that undeliverable addresses can be recorded and not contacted again.

No other mail is read, indexed, copied, analysed or transmitted anywhere. The application never reads message bodies apart from delivery-status notifications.

Limited Use disclosure

email-sender's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the functions described above. It is not transferred to any third party, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train any generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning model. No human reads it other than the account owner.

What is stored, and where

The application runs on a private server controlled by the author. It stores:

  • Recipient email addresses that the author supplies himself, together with an optional name.
  • For each address: whether a message was sent, when, and whether it was rejected or bounced.
  • An OAuth refresh token for the author's own Google Account, held in a file readable only by the service account that runs the application.
  • Operational logs recording sends, failures and errors.

Google account passwords are never requested, handled or stored. Authentication is performed entirely through Google's OAuth 2.0 flow.

What is never done

  • No data is sold, rented, shared or transferred to any third party.
  • No advertising, profiling, tracking pixels or analytics of any kind.
  • No mailing list is built, bought or scraped for resale.
  • No automated decisions are made about anybody.

If you received an email from this application

You were contacted because your address was listed publicly as a hiring or recruiting contact. Reply and ask, and your address will be removed and never contacted again. You may also ask what is held about you, or ask for it to be deleted, by writing to ahmedbenhamida290@gmail.com. Requests are actioned manually, normally within a few days.

Retention

Records are kept only for as long as the author is job-seeking, and exist so that the same person is not contacted twice. They are deleted when the application stops being used, or earlier on request.
